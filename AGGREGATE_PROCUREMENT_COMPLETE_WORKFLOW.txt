CRITICAL BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS & SAFEGUARDS FOR YOUR FLOW
================================================================================

1. PREVENTING SELLER-BUYER DISINTERMEDIATION (BYPASS RISK):
   - Current Code Check: In `POST /api/send-enquiry-email` (lines 2042-2065), you show:
     `Verified Buyer from [Location]` with demand quantity.
   - Recommendation: Keep doing this! Never expose the buyer's real company name, 
     phone number, or direct email to the seller during the RFQ/Quotation stage. 
     If the seller knows the buyer's direct identity, they could bypass Synergy5M. 
     Only disclose shipping delivery address details after the deal is officially booked.

2. RFQ BIDDING WINDOW / COUNTDOWN:
   - Introduce an RFQ submission deadline (e.g. "Quotations close in 48 Hours"). 
   - This creates urgency for sellers to offer their lowest possible bulk rate 
     promptly, keeping buyer wait times short.

3. DUAL-CHANNEL PLATFORM FEE ARCHITECTURE:
   - You already enforced Platform Fee as compulsory in both `AdminAddProduct.jsx` 
     and `VendorSupplyOfferForm.jsx`.
   - Maintain the margin calculation:
     Platform Profit = (Buyer Price - Seller Offered Price) + Success Fee %
